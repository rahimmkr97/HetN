Expertise

Four operations under one roof

Printing, lamination, slitting and bag making are all done in the same plant: one point of contact, from your artwork to the reel or the ready-to-fill bag.

Flexographic printing

Dense solids, sharp text and colours true to your brand, run after run.

  • Up to 8 colours
  • Maximum web width 1160 mm
  • Proof approved before the print run

Lamination

Several films combined for the barrier, stiffness and sealability your product requires.

  • Structures: [DUPLEX / TRIPLEX]
  • Solvent-free lamination
  • Print protected between the layers

Slitting

Reels cut to the exact width of your machines, with even winding.

  • Maximum web width 1350 mm
  • Cores [76 / 152] mm
  • Maximum diameter 400 mm

Bag making

Your films converted into ready-to-fill bags, printed or plain, to your dimensions.

  • Types: [STAND-UP / 3-SIDE SEAL / FLAT BOTTOM…]

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you design our packaging artwork?+

Yes. Our team can design your artwork or adapt your files for flexographic printing. You approve the proof before any print run.

How many colours can you print?+

Up to 8 colours, on a maximum web width of 1160 mm.

Which files should we send?+

Ideally a vector file (PDF, AI or EPS) with text converted to outlines. If you only have a photo or an old pack, we can work from that.

What is the minimum order quantity?+

It depends on the product, the structure and the number of colours. Tell us what you need and we will make you a suitable offer.

What is the production lead time?+

It depends on the quantity and how complex the pack is. We give it to you with the quote.
